Welcome. I'm Dennis, a software engineer based in Jakarta. I spend most of my time on backend systems and payment integrations, and I've decided to start writing some of it down.

## Why I'm writing

I've learned a lot from other engineers' posts, everything from distributed systems to debugging odd production issues. This is my way of giving some of that back and keeping notes on the things I work out along the way. Expect a mix of proper write-ups and the occasional half-formed thought.

## A bit about me

I currently work at **CSG International** on order-management software for telecom and financial services. Before that I was at **Paper.id**, where I built a payment engine that handled around IDR 4 billion in monthly payment volume.

A few things I've worked on:

- A unified payment engine supporting QRIS, virtual accounts, and cross-border remittances
- A 99% transaction success rate on that engine
- Projects that won industry awards, including TM Forum DTW 2025

## What's next

I'll mostly write about backend engineering, payments, and the practical side of keeping systems reliable, plus whatever I happen to be learning. Thanks for reading. More soon.
