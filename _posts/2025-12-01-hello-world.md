Welcome. I'm Dennis, a software engineer based in Jakarta. I spend most of my time on backend systems and payment integrations, and I've decided to start writing some of it down.

## Why I'm writing

I've learned a lot from other engineers' posts, everything from distributed systems to debugging odd production issues. Writing is mostly how I keep notes on the things I work out along the way, and putting them somewhere public makes me actually finish the thought. Expect a mix of proper write-ups and the occasional half-formed one.

## A bit about me

I'm at **CSG International**, working on order-management software for telecom and financial services. Before that I was at **Paper.id**, on backend systems for business payments and financing.

A few things I've worked on:

- A payment engine covering QRIS, virtual accounts, and cross-border remittances, running at a 99% success rate on roughly IDR 4 billion of monthly volume
- An MVP that turned network service requests into API transformation scripts, part of a joint project that won a TM Forum DTW 2025 award
- A paper on sarcasm detection with fine-tuned Llama 2, which won a Best Paper Award at ICAICTA 2024

## What's next

Mostly backend engineering, payments, and the practical side of keeping systems reliable, plus whatever I happen to be learning. Thanks for reading.
